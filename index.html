Executive summary

A P&L-owning commercial leader with 12+ years building and running the business side of content production across India's broadcast, OTT, digital and live-entertainment ecosystem.

As Head of Commercial at Swastik Stories, I control a ₹120 Cr+ annual production P&L across 6+ simultaneous prime-time and digital properties. I built the commercial function of the 25-acre Swastik Bhoomi studio from zero: rate architecture, client acquisition and partnership economics, against a ₹10 Cr+ revenue mandate.

I also lead commercial and financial planning for the studio's new-format slate: AI-hybrid mythology content, a music reality format and self-funded animated IP.

My record is in creating new revenue verticals, restructuring deal terms to protect cashflow, and turning production infrastructure into a business, for content on Sony, Colors, Star Plus, Netflix, Applause and Hotstar.

Six years coordinating India's biggest live and awards formats built the execution depth behind every commercial call I make today.
