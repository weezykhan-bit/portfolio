You're at 3 Instagram followers and 1 Facebook follower. Nothing pays yet; every program below has a follower or invitation gate. The plan is to hit the gates in the right order and avoid getting disqualified on the way.

## Where to check eligibility
- Instagram: Profile → Professional dashboard → Monetization status / "Earn money".
- Facebook: Page → Professional dashboard → Monetization → Overview. It shows each tool, what's missing, and any policy issues.

## Protect eligibility from day one
1. **Originality is your advantage.** Meta's March 2026 originality rules demonetise reposted clips, re-cut highlights and "react" videos. Your episodes are your own graphics and narration, which is exactly what Meta rewards. Never post match footage or agency photos you don't have rights to, even once.
2. **Music: Meta Sound Collection or the in-app library only.** An unlicensed track can block monetization on a Reel.
3. **Label the AI voice.** The narration is synthetic. When posting, turn on "AI info" / "Add AI label" (Advanced settings). Meta asks creators to disclose realistic AI-generated audio, and unlabelled AI content can be restricted.
4. **No betting or real-money fantasy sponsors.** India's 2025 online-gaming law bans promoting real-money games. Football pages get these offers first; turn them down.
5. **Don't buy followers or engagement.** It fails the "authentic, established presence" check in Meta's Partner Monetization Policies.

## Money that doesn't wait for Meta
- **Brand partnerships** (from ~1–2k engaged followers): football boots and kit brands, sports nutrition, football academies and coaching apps, ticketing and travel for match trips. Use Instagram's "Paid partnership" label.
- **Affiliate links** for football books, kits and streaming subscriptions in a link-in-bio page.
- **Sell the format:** the same pipeline makes episodes on any football story in about a day. Clubs, academies and sports media need short-form editorial video.
- **YouTube Shorts:** the same vertical files can go up there too, and YouTube's Partner Program is a separate income stream. Upload only the versions with licensed or no music.

## The growth target that unlocks most of this
~3 episodes a week for 8–12 weeks. Ask a question in the first comment, reply within the first hour, and watch retention. Ep 002 at 100 seconds is the test: if viewers drop off in the dressing-room section, keep future episodes to ~60 seconds.

## Instagram settings to set by hand (no app can change these)
- Settings → Account status: "Can your account be recommended to non-followers?" must show a green tick.
- Settings → Sharing and reuse: remixing ON, resharing to Stories ON, downloads ON, embeds ON.
- Accounts Center → Sharing across profiles: auto-share Reels to the Offside Stories Facebook Page. Once it's on, stop cross-posting manually, or episodes will appear twice.
- Profile: name "Offside Stories | Football Docs", bio from INSTAGRAM-PROFILE.md, category Digital creator, account public.
- Each new Reel: upload the episode cover as its cover, add an in-app track at 15–25%, turn on the AI label, and put the question in the first comment.
