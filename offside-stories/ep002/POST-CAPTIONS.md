## Main caption (recommended)
3–0 down at half-time in a Champions League final. ⚽

Istanbul, 25 May 2005. Maldini scored in the first minute. Crespo added two before the break. Liverpool walked into the dressing room with 45 minutes left and nothing to lose.

Then six minutes changed everything. Gerrard. Šmicer. Alonso. 3–3.

Dudek's double save in extra time. Shootout won 3–2. Champions of Europe.

It's never over until it's over.
