## Facts (checked against the ESPN match report and the match record)
1st leg 14 Feb 2017, Parc des Princes: PSG 4–0 (Di María 18', 55'; Draxler 40'; Cavani 72').
2nd leg 8 Mar 2017, Camp Nou: Suárez 3', Kurzawa own goal 40', Messi pen 50', Cavani 62', Neymar 88' and 90+1' pen, Sergi Roberto 90+5'. Aggregate 6–5. Described as the largest comeback in Champions League history. Luis Enrique before the match: "If they can score four, we can score six."
