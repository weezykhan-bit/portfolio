# LF01 fact sheet: what the script claims, and how sure we are

**Verified** (Wikipedia 2014–15 and 2015–16 Leicester City season pages, LCFC, theScore/ITV):
- Bottom at Christmas 2014 and still bottom in April 2015, 7 points from safety. Won 7 of the last 9 games and finished 14th.
- Promoted as 2013–14 Championship winners.
- Pearson sacked in summer 2015. Ranieri appointed 13 Jul 2015, age 63. Odds 5000–1.
- Ranieri's previous job: Greece lost 0–1 to the Faroe Islands (Nov 2014) and he was sacked. Lineker tweeted "Claudio Ranieri? Really?"
- Kanté signed from Caen on 3 Aug 2015 for a reported £5.6m. Vardy from Fleetwood in 2012 for a reported ~£1m. Mahrez from Le Havre (Ligue 2) in Jan 2014 for a reported ~£400k.
- Results: 4–2 v Sunderland on the opening day. 3–2 v Aston Villa, 13 Sep, from 0–2 down. 2–5 v Arsenal, 26 Sep (home).
- Vardy scored in 11 consecutive Premier League games, the 11th v Man Utd on 28 Nov 2015 (1–1). The previous record was 10, held by Van Nistelrooy.
- Top at Christmas 2015. Lost 1–0 at Liverpool on 26 Dec (league defeat 2).
- Won 3–1 at Man City on 6 Feb 2016 (Huth 2, Mahrez; Agüero scored City's goal).
- Lost 2–1 at Arsenal on 14 Feb 2016: Vardy pen, Simpson sent off, Walcott, Welbeck 90+5'. It was their last league defeat.
- Leicester 1–0 Southampton, 3 Apr (Morgan header). Leicester 2–2 West Ham, 17 Apr (Vardy sent off, Ulloa stoppage-time pen). Leicester 4–0 Swansea, 24 Apr.
- Man Utd 1–1 Leicester, 1 May. Chelsea 2–2 Tottenham, 2 May: Spurs led 2–0 at half-time, then goals from Cahill and Hazard. Spurs received 9 yellow cards. The players watched at Vardy's house.
- 7 May: Leicester 3–1 Everton (Vardy 2, King). Bocelli sang Nessun Dorma.
- Final table: Leicester 81, Arsenal 71, Tottenham 70, Man City 66. 3 league defeats.
- Vardy: 24 PL goals, FWA Footballer of the Year. Mahrez: PFA Players' Player of the Year, the first African winner.
- Kanté joined Chelsea and won the 2016–17 title. Leicester reached the 2016–17 Champions League quarter-finals. Ranieri sacked 23 Feb 2017.
- Vichai Srivaddhanaprabha (b. 1958) died on 27 Oct 2018 when his helicopter crashed outside the stadium after the West Ham game.
- Vardy won the 2019–20 Golden Boot at 33. FA Cup won in 2021, the club's first.

**Anecdotes, widely reported:** pizza for the first clean sheet (late Oct 2015, the players made the pizzas); "dilly ding, dilly dong"; the 40-point target.
