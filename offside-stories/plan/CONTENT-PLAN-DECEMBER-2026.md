# Offside Stories: December 2026 content plan (YouTube-first)

**The model:** one YouTube documentary a week. Every Reel is cut from that week's documentary, and Instagram posts carry the topics that work better as slides than as video. Facebook gets everything without extra work: YouTube long-forms are uploaded to it natively, and Instagram Reels and posts reach it through Instagram's auto-share.

## 1. Weekly rhythm (IST)

| Day | What goes out | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Sun** | Long-form documentary, 8–10 min, 16:9 | YouTube + Facebook (native upload) |
| **Mon · Wed · Fri** | Reel cut from that long-form, 40–60 s, 9:16 | Instagram Reel + YouTube Short (same file); Facebook via auto-share |
| **Tue · Thu** | Instagram post (carousel or single card), 4:5 | Instagram; Facebook via auto-share |
| Sat | Long-form is produced and scheduled (nothing posts) | — |

Exception: **Messi vs Ronaldo** goes out on **Fri 18 Dec**, the anniversary of the 2022 World Cup final.

## 4. What "shots" means here
No camera filming: every shot is a designed scene (no match footage, no broadcast audio). The shot types are:

| Shot type | Example |
|---|---|
| Scoreboard / live score | Chelsea 0–0 → 2–2 Tottenham, clock, events list |
| Stat stamp | "5000–1", "£5.6m", "+10" |
| Photo + namecard | Licensed Commons photo, tinted, Ken Burns move, name bar |
| Graphic panel | When no licensed photo exists: big outlined word over texture |
| Table / ladder | League position, final table bars |
| Timeline / route | Career path, tournament bracket, "after" timeline |
| Tactics board | Formation, passing arrow |
| Quote card | Verified quote with attribution |
| Chapter card | "Chapter 03 · The bargains" |
| Brand sign-off | OFFSIDE STORIES · Beyond the 90 minutes |

## 5. Shot list per long-form

**Every long-form (~45 shots for 8–10 min, one new visual about every 11 s):**
- Cold open, 3–4 shots: the peak moment (scoreboard or clock), the stakes, the shock number, the title over a photo.
- Chapters, 8–10 of them. Each gets a chapter card plus 3–4 story shots, roughly one per narration beat.
- Close, 3 shots: the question card, a recap line, the brand sign-off.

## 7. Automation
A scheduled session runs daily at 15:12 IST and follows `plan/AUTOMATION-RUNBOOK.md` on the queue `plan/content-queue.csv`:
- **Make and post whatever is due.** Long-forms are produced the day before, uploaded privately to YouTube and scheduled on Facebook, then made public at the slot.
- **Check before posting, to avoid duplicates.** It looks at each platform first and skips anything already live.
- **Report each run.** It sends a push notification with the links.

**Facts are its only safety net.** Every claim needs a source read during that run, and anything unconfirmed is cut. There is no human review before posting, so a wrong fact can still go live. Check the run reports. To pause the automation, tell Claude "pause the Offside Stories automation".

**Do by hand once:**
- Instagram → Accounts Center → share Reels and posts to Facebook automatically.
- Turn on the AI label for Reels in the Instagram app. The API can't set it.
- Set "altered or synthetic content" to Yes on each YouTube video.
