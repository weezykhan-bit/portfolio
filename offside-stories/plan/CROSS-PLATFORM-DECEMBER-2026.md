## 1. The model: one big story a week, cut into pieces

```
Weekend LONG-FORM (10–12 min, 16:9)  → YouTube + Facebook
        │
        ├── 2–3 cut-downs (40–60 s, 9:16) → Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts + Facebook Reels
        ├── 1 carousel (6–8 slides)        → Instagram + Facebook (+ YouTube Post)
        └── 1 trailer Reel (≤60 s)         → posted 1–2 days before the long-form
+ 1–2 standalone Shorts from the December topic list
+ 1 quote/stat card
+ daily Stories (polls, quizzes, teasers)
```

Why this works: research is the expensive part, so one researched story feeds 4–5 posts. Every Short is linked to its long-form through YouTube's "Related video" field, so Short viewers flow into the long-form, where watch hours count.

**Each long-form, chapter by chapter** (~1,500 words at ~145 words a minute):
- **0:00–0:30 cold open:** the most dramatic moment first ("3–0 down at half-time…").
- **0:30–2:30 world:** who they were, what was at stake.
- **2:30–5:30 rise or build-up:** three to four key beats, each with one stat or graphic.
- **5:30–8:30 turning point and climax:** played at match pace, with clocks and scoreboards.
- **8:30–10:30 legacy:** what changed, where they are now.
- **Close:** a question for the comments, then the Offside Stories end screen with the next video.

Keep it **over 8 minutes** so mid-roll ads are possible once you're monetized. Add **chapters** (timestamps) to every description.

**Visuals for long-form:** the same graphics system, but horizontal. Add the elements that only work over 10 minutes: animated maps (cities, routes), league tables moving week by week, tactical boards, quote cards, "where are they now" panels and AI atmosphere B-roll (stadiums, crowds, silhouettes). **No match footage, no broadcast audio, no AI-generated real players.**

**Thumbnails (long-form):** one huge number or word plus the brand colours, max 3 words: e.g. "5000–1", "3–0 DOWN", "MESSI v RONALDO". It's the same look as the Reel covers, in 16:9. Custom thumbnails need the channel phone-verified.

## 7. What success looks like by 31 Dec (targets, not promises)

| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Long-form average view duration | ≥ 35% (≈4 min of 11) | It decides whether YouTube recommends the video |
| Long-form click-through rate (thumbnail) | ≥ 4% | Below that, change the thumbnail, not the video |
| Short → long-form clicks | Track "Related video" clicks per Short | Shows whether the funnel works |
| Subscribers | 500 (unlocks YouTube fan funding together with 3,000 watch hours) | First monetization step |
| Watch hours | Track weekly; the bar is **8,000 h** for applications from 1 Feb 2027 | ≈120k long-form views at 4 min each |
