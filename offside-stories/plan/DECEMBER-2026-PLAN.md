Episodes continue from 003 (001 Vardy and 002 Istanbul went out on 27 Nov). December 2026 starts on a Tuesday and has exactly 23 weekdays, so 23 topics fill every weekday, including Christmas Day.

| #10 Messi vs Ronaldo | **Moved to Fri 18 Dec** | 18 Dec is the anniversary of the 2022 World Cup final. It's the one natural news hook in the month. |

## Visual approach (read before producing)
- **The backbone is graphics-led storytelling:** scoreboards, timelines, pitch diagrams, name cards and big type, as in Episodes 001–002. It's fast, consistent, cheap and clearly *original*, which Meta's 2026 monetization rules reward.
- **AI video (Kling/Runway) is for atmosphere only:** empty stadiums at night, crowd silhouettes, rain on a pitch, a tunnel, a lone figure seen from behind. **Never generate a recognisable real player.** The tools distort famous faces, it misleads viewers, and it carries likeness risk. Never generate a crest; use team colours and names.
- **Kits:** describe them in graphics (colour chips, e.g. Milan in *white* in the 2005 final). Don't render them on fake players.
- **Label AI:** turn on Instagram's AI label for any Reel with AI video or the AI voice.
- **Length discipline:** Ep 002 ran 100 s. December documentaries stay at **45–60 s**. Check Ep 002's retention graph before starting week 1.

## DELIVERABLE 2 — Episode development

For each: **title · hook · summary · emotional angle · most surprising fact · central conflict · why people share · facts to verify · duration/format**. "Verify" means check against at least two reliable sources (UEFA/FIFA/club records, BBC Sport, Guardian, ESPN, Opta) before scripting. Anything marked *disputed* must be presented as disputed on screen.

### 003 · Leicester: The 5000–1 Miracle (DOC 55 s)
- **Summary:** escaped relegation in 2014–15, appointed Ranieri, and with Vardy, Mahrez and Kanté became 2015–16 champions.
- **Angle:** a cheap squad that played like a family. **Surprise:** bookmakers offered better odds on stranger events than on a Leicester title. **Conflict:** the system vs. a team built from rejects. **Share:** "the most impossible title ever".
- **Verify:** 5000–1 pre-season odds; title confirmed 2 May 2016 when Tottenham drew 2–2 at Chelsea; 81 points; Vardy 24 PL goals; Mahrez PFA Player of the Year 2016; the "great escape" (7 wins in the last 9 games of 2014–15, under Nigel Pearson).

### 004 · Barcelona 6–1 PSG (VIRAL 40 s)
- **Summary:** lost 4–0 in Paris; 3–1 up at 88' needing three more because of Cavani's away goal; Neymar free kick 88', Neymar penalty 90+1', Sergi Roberto 90+5'.
- **Surprise:** they needed three goals in about seven minutes. **Conflict:** the away-goals arithmetic. **Share:** disbelief.
- **Verify:** 8 Mar 2017 (2nd leg), 14 Feb 2017 (1st leg 4–0); exact goal minutes; "first team to overturn a four-goal first-leg deficit in the Champions League".

### 005 · Greece 2004 (VIRAL 40 s)
- **Summary:** Otto Rehhagel's Greece beat hosts Portugal in the opener and the final; Charisteas header in the final.
- **Surprise:** Greece had never won a match at a major tournament before Euro 2004. **Conflict:** defence-first football vs. a golden generation (Figo, Ronaldo at 19). **Share:** the ultimate underdog.
- **Verify:** pre-tournament odds (quoted variously as 80–1 to 150–1; use "around 150–1" only if sourced); results 2–1 Portugal (group), 1–0 France (QF), silver-goal 1–0 Czech Republic (SF), 1–0 Portugal (final, 4 July 2004, Charisteas 57'); the "never won a match at a major tournament" claim.

## DELIVERABLE 7 — Performance tracker

Use `performance-tracker.csv` (one row per post; fill it 48 hours after posting from Professional Dashboard → the Reel → Insights).

**Derived metrics (normalise for reach):**
- **Shares per 1,000 views** and **saves per 1,000 views**: the clearest signal of value.
- **Completion** = average watch time ÷ duration.
- **Follow rate** = new followers ÷ profile visits.

**Starting targets** (your own baselines after week 2 replace these):
- 3-second hold above 60%
- completion above 40% for docs and above 55% for viral and debate
- shares above 10 per 1,000 views

**Reading the pillars (every Friday):**
1. Average each metric **by pillar and by format** (not by single post; one outlier misleads).
2. A pillar is **working** if it beats your account median on shares/1k *or* follow rate for two consecutive weeks.
3. **Low 3-second hold** → fix the hook and the first frame. **Good hold but low completion** → the middle is too long; cut 10–15 seconds. **Good completion but no shares** → the ending lacks a feeling or a question.
4. In January, give the winning pillar 2 of the 5 weekly slots and cut the weakest one to every other week.

## Editorial standards checklist (every episode)
- [ ] Every date, score, scorer and stat checked against 2 reliable sources, with sources noted in the script file
- [ ] Disputed points labelled "disputed" or attributed ("Materazzi later said…")
- [ ] No invented dialogue, dressing-room speeches or private quotes
- [ ] No AI-generated real players, crests or "archival-looking" fake footage
- [ ] Kits and colours correct for that match
- [ ] Tragedies: no recreations of violence or collapses, no sensational sound, no polls
- [ ] AI label on; music from the in-app library only
