# Portfolio film: three storylines

Audience is a recruiter or hiring leader who knows nothing about Fowzan yet. The thing they should remember: "he owns the numbers and knows how a shoot actually runs."
All figures come from `index.html` (2026 résumé). The film shows no promo footage from rights holders, no headshot and no invented claims.

Assumptions: 1920×1080, 30–40 s, music plus a few sound effects from free sources, no voiceover, nothing paid. Colours and fonts are the site's own (navy #0D1B2E, report stock #F3F3EF, vermilion #C9381C, Schibsted Grotesk and Source Serif 4).

---

## A. The ledger behind the shot (recommended)

**Device:** a number lands. **Form:** one frame that keeps rebuilding.
A shoot-day call sheet on report-stock paper fills in line by line: crew, location, cost codes. Each block then turns into the business figure it stands for, so the viewer sees the floor and the P&L as one page.

- **First 2 s:** a call-sheet header stamps in: "SHOOT DAY · 6 SHOWS ON AIR". The cost column ticks down and "6+" lifts out of it in vermilion, as large as the frame allows.
- **Beats:** call sheet → "6+ properties at once" → the location row expands into a 25-square lot grid, "25 acres, built from zero" → the grid fills with revenue, "₹10 Cr+ mandate" → the contact column scrolls to "3,000+ decision-makers" → the sheet folds into a report cover: "12+ years. Coordinator to Head of Commercial" → end card with name, title, email and LinkedIn.
- **Look:** report-stock ground with navy type and hairline rules. Vermilion is used for one figure at a time only. The material is a printed ledger.
- **Sound:** a low, steady pulse with pen and stamp hits on each figure, and one clean resolve on the end card.

## B. Twelve sheets

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** one continuous take along a timeline.
Calendar sheets tear off from 2014 to 2026. Each year carries one role and one named show, and the camera never cuts. The pace speeds up as he moves from coordinating to running the business.

- **First 2 s:** a sheet reading "2014 · Production Coordinator · Filmfare Awards" tears away to reveal 2016.
- **Beats:** coordinator years (Sa Re Ga Ma Pa, Zee Cine Awards) → Executive Producer (AK vs AK campaign) → OTT slate (Illegal S2, Code M S2) → Contiloe (Taj S2) → 2024 sheet lands hard: "Head of Commercial" → key figures stack up on the last sheet → end card.
- **Look:** the site's tear-off pad, navy ground and report-stock sheets.
- **Sound:** paper tears on each year, with a rising tempo.

## C. Twenty-five acres (the bold one)

**Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** a flight through one world.
A top-down 25-square plan of Swastik Bhoomi. The camera flies low across it, and each plot it passes switches on as a revenue stream (web series, micro-drama, YouTube creators, corporate events…) until the empty lot runs as a business.

- **First 2 s:** an empty grid of 25 grey squares with the label "25 acres. No commercial function." One square turns vermilion.
- **Beats:** empty lot → rate card with margin floors stamps onto plot 1 → plots light as streams → outreach lines fan out to "3,000+" → pull up to the full lit lot: "₹10 Cr+ mandate" → "and 6+ shows on air beside it" → end card.
- **Look:** an architectural site plan, navy linework on report stock.
- **Sound:** a soft, airy flight bed with a click as each plot comes on.

C is the most striking of the three but only covers the studio part of the career. B is the safest but reads like a CV. A carries the actual positioning: floor and finance together.
